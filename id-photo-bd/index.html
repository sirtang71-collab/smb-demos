🇧🇩 Bangladesh ID Photo Maker

Bangladesh applications reject photos that miss the pixel size or the file-size limit. This maker crops your photo to the exact pixels and lands the file inside the exact KB window — NID, e-Passport, admission and job applications.

🔒 100% processed on your device. No upload, no server, no tracking.

Presets follow commonly published guidance: NID photo 300×300 px · white background · ≤100 KB · e-Passport 2×2 in = 600×600 px · University / govt job photo 300×300 px · ≤100 KB. Every KB window is editable — always check the latest official notice for your application.

How a minimum KB is reached honestly (when your notice sets one): first JPEG quality is raised to the maximum that fits. If the file is still below the minimum, standard JPEG padding data is added — your picture itself is never altered, blurred or noised, and the export message tells you when padding was used. If a file would need absurd amounts of padding (a tiny thumbnail instead of a real photo), the tool refuses and tells you why, instead of handing you a file the portal would reject.

Your photo never leaves this device. Everything runs locally in your browser. No uploads, no accounts, no tracking.
