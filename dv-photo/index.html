🇺🇸 US DV Lottery Photo Maker

The DV entry system rejects the file itself when the photo is the wrong size or the wrong file size. This tool crops your photo to exactly 600×600 px, saves it as a JPEG of 240 KB or less, and walks you through the head-size and background checks — with a plain white or off-white background, head 50–69% of the frame.

🔒 100% processed on your device. No upload, no server, no tracking.

Digital photo: exactly 600×600 px · square · colour JPEG · ≤240 KB · head 50–69% of frame height (chin to crown) · eyes 56–69% up from the bottom · plain white or off-white background · taken within the last 6 months · no glasses.

⚠️ Read first — US 2026 rule: the State Department does not accept photos altered with computer software, phone apps, filters or AI — including replacing or whitening the background, skin smoothing, lighting changes and AI sharpening. A manipulated photo may make you ineligible. This tool only crops, resizes and re-encodes — the same three things the Department's own free photo tool does. It does not change your background and does not retouch your face. Take the photo against a plain white or off-white wall, in daylight, facing the camera.

1 · Add your photo

Use the original full-size photo from your camera or phone — not a thumbnail, screenshot or chat-app copy. The photo must be recent (within the last 6 months) and must not be the photo from a previous year's entry.

2 · Position your head — drag & zoom

Your photo will appear here. Drag to move it.

┄ red box = biggest allowed head (69%) · ┄ green box = smallest allowed head (50%) · blue band = where the eyes must fall (56–69% up from the bottom)
Your head must fill the green box and stay inside the red box: the crown sits in the band between the two boxes at the top, the chin in the band between them at the bottom.

Measure the head (in the 600 px frame)

Read the positions off the preview above — 0 is the top edge of the frame, 600 is the bottom. The tool does the maths; you confirm it looks right.

Enter the crown and chin to check the head size (must be 50–69% of the 600 px frame).

Enter the eye line to check the eye height (must be 56–69% up from the bottom of the frame).

How to eyeball it: the head, chin to crown including the hair, should take up about half to two-thirds of the picture — a little more than half, a little less than three-quarters. If the head nearly touches the top and bottom edges, it is too big; if there is a lot of empty space above the head and below the shoulders, it is too small. Eyes sit a little above the middle of the frame.

How the 240 KB ceiling is reached honestly: the photo is encoded at the highest JPEG quality that still fits under 240,000 bytes. Nothing is added or removed from the picture — no padding, no filters, no retouching. If even the lowest quality is over the ceiling (very noisy originals), the tool refuses instead of handing you a file the entry system would reject.
