    ⬇ Download compressed PDF

    What this tool does with your file: it reads your PDF in your browser, recompresses the embedded images (JPEG images are re-encoded; other image data is decoded and re-encoded as JPEG), and rebuilds the PDF with the same pages and page sizes — nothing leaves your device at any point. Password-protected (encrypted) PDFs cannot be compressed; remove the password first and try again. Text-only PDFs have no images to shrink and will be refused with a clear message — they are already as small as they can be. A few very old or unusually compressed PDFs may be refused with a clear message instead of producing a broken file.
