    Split mode

    One file per range: “1-3, 5, 7-9” makes three files — pages 1–3, page 5, and pages 7–9. Page numbers start at 1.

    Chops the file into equal chunks — every 2 pages makes one file per pair (the last file takes whatever is left).

    Each page becomes its own file, named after its page number — “1, 3, 5” makes split-page-1.pdf, split-page-3.pdf and split-page-5.pdf. Ranges work too: “2-4” pulls out pages 2, 3 and 4 separately.

      What this tool does with your file: it reads your PDF in your browser, copies the pages you picked into new PDFs, and hands you the results — nothing leaves your device at any point. Password-protected (encrypted) PDFs cannot be split; remove the password first and try again. A few very old or unusually compressed PDFs may be refused with a clear message instead of producing a broken file.
