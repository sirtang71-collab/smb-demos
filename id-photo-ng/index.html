🇳🇬 Nigeria ID Photo Maker

Nigerian applications reject photos that miss the pixel size or the file-size limit. This maker crops your photo to the exact pixels and lands the file inside the exact KB window — NIS e-Passport portal, passport print, and university admission.

🔒 100% processed on your device. No upload, no server, no tracking.

Presets follow published guidance: NIS portal 600×800 px · white background · under 2 MB · Passport print 35×45 mm = 413×531 px · white background · University admission JPEG · white background · ≤20 KB (one published example). Every KB window is editable — always check the latest official notice for your application.

JAMB note: UTME registration no longer accepts a passport-photo upload — since the 2026 guidelines JAMB captures your photo live at the CBT centre. This tool does not offer a JAMB preset for that reason; use the passport presets above for other applications.

How a minimum KB is reached honestly (when your notice sets one): first JPEG quality is raised to the maximum that fits. If the file is still below the minimum, standard JPEG padding data is added — your picture itself is never altered, blurred or noised, and the export message tells you when padding was used. If a file would need absurd amounts of padding (a tiny thumbnail instead of a real photo), the tool refuses and tells you why, instead of handing you a file the portal would reject.

Your photo never leaves this device. Everything runs locally in your browser. No uploads, no accounts, no tracking.
