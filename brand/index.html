Our story

Started by engineers who kept replacing cheap chargers

VOLTIQ was founded in Shenzhen in 2019 by two hardware engineers from the power-supply industry. The idea was unglamorous: the chargers sold across Southeast Asia's marketplaces were failing in the heat — swollen batteries, dead ports, cables that lasted a season. Nobody was engineering for the climate people actually live in.

So we did. Every VOLTIQ design starts with three constraints: heat, humidity, and unstable voltage. Components are derated for 40°C ambient, boards are conformal-coated against moisture, and every charger carries surge protection that has saved more laptops than we can count.

Today VOLTIQ sells through official brand stores on Shopee Mall, LazMall and TikTok Shop in six markets, and through authorised distributors offline. We would rather grow one market properly than ten markets thinly.
